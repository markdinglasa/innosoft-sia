# Master Software Development Agreement (MSDA)

This Master Software Development Agreement establishes the overarching legal and business terms governing all work performed by the Developer for the Client. 

## 1. Intellectual Property & Data Ownership
All business, transactional, customer, employee, financial, and other data supplied by the Client remains the exclusive property of the Client. 

Upon full payment of all milestones defined in any applicable Statement of Work (SOW), intellectual property rights shall be distributed as follows:

**Client Owns:**
- Custom application source code specifically written for the integration (after full payment).
- Custom report definitions and specific XML mappings.
- Client-specific documentation.
- All Client Data (at all times).

**Developer Retains:**
- Pre-existing code, generic libraries, and open-source frameworks.
- Reusable, generic architecture components (e.g., standard data extraction utilities).
- Development methodologies and general technical know-how.

## 2. Payment Terms
Payments for any project are tied to the successful completion and formal sign-off of specific milestones defined in the applicable SOW.
*Invoices are strictly due within Net-15 days of milestone completion. Work on subsequent milestones may pause if prior milestone invoices are unresolved.*

## 3. Legacy System Dependencies & Liability
The Developer's ability to perform the Services is dependent upon the availability, accessibility, reliability, and compatibility of the Client's legacy system and related data sources. 

**The Developer shall not be responsible for delays caused by unavailable systems, inaccurate data, undocumented legacy behavior, infrastructure failures, restricted access, or changes made to the legacy system by the Client or third parties.**

## 4. Warranty 
**Warranty Period**
The Developer provides a **30-day warranty** commencing immediately after Production Deployment for any given SOW. During this period, the Developer will fix, at no additional cost, any defects that cause the software to fail to meet the agreed requirements outlined in the SOW and SRS. 
*Note: The warranty does not cover issues caused by changes in the legacy database, infrastructure failures, or external network outages.*

## 5. Change Management & Regulatory Clause
Any request that adds to or modifies the Scope of Work will be subject to a formal **Change Request (CR)** process. Work on new requests will not commence until the CR is formally approved and signed by all stakeholders.

**Regulatory & Accreditation Change Clause:**
Requirements introduced, modified, or imposed by a regulatory body or accrediting authority *after* the initial approval of the Requirements Specification shall be treated as a change in scope unless expressly included in the original SOW guidelines.
