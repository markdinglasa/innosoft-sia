# Support & Maintenance Agreement

This agreement governs post-deployment services after the initial 30-day warranty period defined in the MSDA has expired. 

## 1. Included Services (If under active retainer)
Maintenance & Support is a separate paid service that covers:
- Bug fixes outside the warranty window.
- Infrastructure monitoring and database maintenance.
- Security updates and general user support.

*Note: Major new features or new report types require a separate Statement of Work (SOW).*

## 2. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
When an issue is reported, it will be classified by the Developer and responded to according to the following SLAs:

| Severity Level | Description | Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | System down entirely, EOD cannot be generated, preventing store closure. | 4 Business Hours |
| **High** | Major feature broken, TMS rejecting XML due to sudden structural changes. | 1 Business Day |
| **Medium** | Specific non-blocking bugs, data discrepancies requiring investigation. | 2 Business Days |
| **Low** | Cosmetic issues, general inquiries, minor log formatting requests. | 5 Business Days |

## 3. Out of Scope for Maintenance
- Recovery from hardware failure or client-side network destruction.
- Rewriting the application due to complete legacy database replacement by the client.
