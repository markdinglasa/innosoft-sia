# Accreditation Responsibility Matrix

To successfully achieve Mall TMS Accreditation, both Developer and Client hold specific responsibilities. Final approval, certification, accreditation, or acceptance remains the responsibility of the applicable regulatory/accrediting authority (Mall Admin) and cannot be unilaterally guaranteed by the Developer.

## 1. Developer Responsibilities
- **Technical Implementation**: Implement technical requirements communicated before development.
- **Documentation**: Prepare required technical documentation for the Mall Admin.
- **Environment**: Provide a test environment for XML generation during the testing phase.
- **Evaluation Support**: Assist during the technical evaluation phase.
- **Defect Resolution**: Fix defects attributable to the agreed implementation.
- **Technical Liaison**: Provide reasonable assistance in responding to technical findings from the Mall Admin.

## 2. Client Responsibilities
- **Business Requirements**: Provide accurate business requirements and the latest official TMS guidelines.
- **Access**: Provide legacy-system access and realistic test data.
- **Communication**: Coordinate and communicate directly with the accrediting authority (Mall Admin).
- **Financial Obligations**: Pay any and all accreditation, testing, or regulatory fees.
- **Representation**: Attend formal accreditation meetings with the Mall Admin.
- **Approvals**: Obtain necessary business/legal approvals to proceed with the integration.
